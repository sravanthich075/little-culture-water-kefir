A tiny introduction

So, what’s
water kefir?

Think of it as a light, sparkling drink with a little more story. Water kefir is made by fermenting water and sugar with live kefir cultures. The cultures transform the sweetened water into a gently tangy, naturally fizzy drink.

We make ours in small batches, give it the time it needs, and bottle it cold for your fridge. Every batch can have its own little personality—that’s the lovely thing about fermentation.

What does water kefir taste like?+

It’s usually light, gently tangy, and softly fizzy. The exact taste can vary a little from batch to batch because it’s naturally fermented.

Is water kefir the same as milk kefir?+

No. Water kefir is made by fermenting sweetened water with water kefir cultures. It is not a milk-based kefir drink.

How should I store my bottle?+

Keep it refrigerated and enjoy it cold. Open carefully over a sink, as natural fermentation can create fizz and pressure. Follow the use-by guidance on your bottle.

Does it contain sugar or caffeine?+

It’s made with sugar, which the cultures use during fermentation; the amount remaining can vary by batch. It’s naturally caffeine-free. Please message us if you need ingredient or allergen details.
